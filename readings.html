Literary Reflections

Notes on books that keep pressing on questions of personhood, work, longing, and moral imagination.

🪲
Fiction
Franz Kafka
The Metamorphosis
"Even after waking up as a bug, Gregor's first thoughts are not about fear or freedom, but about work — missing the train, disappointing his employer."
A meditation on usefulness, isolation, and quiet replacement — disturbingly familiar inside high-pressure institutions.
★★★★★

Speculative Fiction
Kazuo Ishiguro
Never Let Me Go
"An exploration of identity, humanity, and the ethics of scientific advancement in a hauntingly familiar world."
A book I keep returning to when thinking about bioethics, compliance, and the governance of science.
★★★★★
🌙
Novella
Fyodor Dostoevsky
White Nights
A brief, tender study of longing, imagined lives, and the strange intimacy of being understood for one night.
What stayed with me is hope treated not as heroic force, but as something fragile and self-deceiving.
★★★★★
